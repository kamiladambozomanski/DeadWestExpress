# Dead West Express

*Developed in collaboration with the University of South Wales.*

## About the Game

Play as **Sheriff Dann**, a cowboy fighting to save his city in an alternate take on America's Golden Age. When a time-travelling anomaly brings zombies into the modern world, chaos spreads across the city. Take up arms, fight back against the undead, and reclaim your home.

**Dead West Express** is a short, six-week game development project created by a team of five as part of a university Tower Defence module.

- ## Disclaimer

*Dead West Express* incorporates elements of political satire, including satirical portrayals of real-world political figures. These elements are intended for comedic and critical effect and are separate from the game's central zombie-survival premise.
